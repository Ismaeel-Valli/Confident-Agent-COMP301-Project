# Source snapshot notes

The bundled provider specifications and curated subsets are the pinned contract inputs to this draft. `bundled_manifest.json` records their SHA-256 hashes. `manifest.json` records the original source retrievals, including documentation pages consulted during curation. Those documentation HTML pages are not redistributed in this package; follow their recorded URLs when reviewing the curated contracts.

Open-Meteo JSON files were converted from the bundled original YAML documents. Their original URLs and YAML hashes are retained. GitHub authentication and the API-version header are additionally grounded in https://docs.github.com/en/rest/authentication/authenticating-to-the-rest-api (consulted 2026-09-21).

GitHub's source license is in `github-license.txt`; Petstore's in `petstore-license.txt`; Open-Meteo's in `openmeteo-license.txt`. Retain their notices when sharing the source specifications. The three `.curated.openapi.json` files are researcher-authored partial representations, not official downloadable provider specifications or independently reviewed ground truth.
